# MyMomentous Privacy — in plain language

MyMomentous is a family money app. Here is what we do with information, written the way we'd explain it across a kitchen table.

## Your kid's information belongs to you

- Kids under 13 never give us an email address. They don't need one — no account of their own is required, and never a real name. "M" or a nickname works fine.
- A kid's record in our system is **the parent's data**. The parent creates it, controls it, and can delete it, entirely and at any time.
- We do not track kids. No advertising, no analytics profiles, no selling data, no third-party trackers in kid-facing screens. Ever.

## What we actually store

- Your household's economy: member names (as you typed them), the pretend-currency ledger, jobs, store items, and goals.
- The parent's sign-in (Sign in with Apple), which shares only what Apple lets you share.
- That's it. Mints are not money; the ledger is a family game until you say otherwise.

## What we never do

- Sell or share your family's data.
- Show ads.
- Message your kid.
- Keep anything after you delete your household.

## Where it lives

Data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) with row-level security: each family's data is technically inaccessible to any other family, enforced by the database itself — not just by app code.

## Questions or deletion requests

Email us and we'll answer in plain language, usually within two days. Deleting your household removes every member, every account, and every transaction.

*This page ships with the first build on purpose. Privacy posture is a day-one feature, not a launch-week patch.*
